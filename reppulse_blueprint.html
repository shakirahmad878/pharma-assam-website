1. Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

RepPulse Pharma SFA is a purpose-built field force automation application engineered specifically for pharmaceutical companies operating in tiered distribution networks (Assam, Barak Valley, Tripura & Northeast India). It eliminates manual reporting, buddy punching, and field reporting leakages through hardware-verified geotagged attendance, 15-minute background location telemetry, doctor call auditing (DCR), and Tour Plan (MTP) governance.

3. Core Operational Modules

3.1 Anti-Fraud Geotagged Attendance & Shift Lock

  • Geotagged Front-Facing Selfie: Captures fresh front-camera photo with real-time timestamping. Gallery selection is prohibited.
  • Hardware GPS Lock & Anti-Mock Engine: Inspects Android location provider flags (isMockLocation). Developer option location spoofing triggers immediate lockout.
  • 10:30 AM Shift Cutoff: Reps attempting to begin duty past 10:30 AM must submit a reason (e.g. clinic delay, transport issue). The request routes instantly to RSM, HR, and Director terminals for authorization.

3.2 15-Minute Background Location Telemetry & Live Map

  • Background Telemetry: An optimized background worker captures rep GPS coordinates and battery percentage every 15 minutes during active shift hours.
  • Real-time Manager Map: RSMs and managers can open Team & Employees → Live Location to track active reps on an interactive map. If a rep has not started their shift, the system displays 'Shift Not Started'.

3.3 Daily Call Reporting (DCR) with 100-Meter Geofencing

  • Clinic Proximity Enforcement: Check-in is permitted only within 100 meters of the doctor's registered GPS coordinate.
  • Brand Detailing & Sampling: Logs discussed brands, sample drops (with batch numbers and quantities), and promotional materials.
  • Chemist POB Booking: Reps capture chemist orders, stockist tags, and booking values on the spot.

3.4 Monthly Tour Programme (MTP) & Deviation Control

  • Pre-Planned Monthly Beats: Reps follow an approved calendar of daily routes across divisions.
  • Mandatory Reschedule Approval: Reps cannot swap dates unilaterally. Any change requires submitting a formal request to the RSM.

3.5 Conflict-Free Offline Synchronization

  • Zero-Data-Loss Architecture: Offline records queue locally in encrypted storage.
  • Deterministic Collision-Resistant IDs: Generates unique identifiers (e.g., att_in_NLCL-0004_2026-11-02) to guarantee idempotent cloud synchronization.
