  • Bodrud RSM will approve instantly from his console to unlock your day.

Module 3 Monthly Tour Programme (MTP)
05 / 10
Tour Planning

Checking Your Approved Tour Plan (MTP)

Your monthly work schedule is live in the cloud and guides your daily calls.

A

Where to Find Your Tour Plan

  • Open the app and tap the "More" (☰) tab on the bottom bar.
  • Select "Tour Plan" (MTP Calendar).
  • You will see your monthly calendar grid with color-coded days:
    • Green (TOUR): Approved working beat.
    • Amber (SUNDAY): Weekly rest day.
    • Rose (LEAVE/HOLIDAY): Official holiday.
  • Tap any date to view scheduled town, target doctors (10), and target chemists (4).
📱 Bottom Bar ➔ More (☰) ➔ Tour Plan
B

How It Syncs to Morning Attendance

  • When you punch in each morning, RepPulse automatically checks the cloud tour_plans table.
  • Your scheduled town (e.g. "Work at Kalain Ex-station") is automatically selected for you!
  • No need to manually remember which beat you are supposed to work today.
  • Rescheduling: If heavy rain or road closure requires changing your beat, use the "Request Reschedule" button to alert Bodrud RSM.
⚡ Auto-Linked between MTP ↔ Attendance
Satyam's & Pranjal's Nov 20-30 tour programs are fully loaded in the database.

Add Products with Rates

The app shows real-time commercial pricing:

  • PTR: Price to Retailer (Chemist buying rate).

Review & Submit Order

Module 7 Travel & Expense Claims

Compensation & Claims

Daily Travel Allowance (TA) & Daily Allowance (DA)

Automatic fare calculations backed by the company Standard Fare Chart (SFC).

A

How SFC Auto-Calculates Your Claim

No manual math needed! When you select your working beat, your entitlement is pre-set:

Work Type Standard Fare (TA) Daily Allowance (DA)
HQ Working (Local) ₹30 - ₹45 ₹250 / day
Ex-station (Kalain/Sonai/Bagha) ₹135 - ₹195 ₹450 / day
Outstation (Halflong/Dharmanagar) ₹420 - ₹540 ₹750 / day
⚙️ Pre-configured in Supabase sfc_routes
B

Submitting Your Daily Expense

  • Go to More (☰) ➔ Expenses.
  • Tap "Claim Today's Expense".
  • Select the route you worked. The base TA & DA are filled in automatically.
  • Add extra costs if applicable:
    • Hotel / Lodge charges (for Outstation overnight stays).
    • Toll / Parking / Courier tickets.
  • Attach photo of ticket or receipt.
  • Tap "Submit Claim" ➔ Status displays as Pending Approval until RSM approves.
📱 More (☰) ➔ Expenses ➔ Add Expense
Submit expenses on the same evening to ensure fast weekly clearance.
