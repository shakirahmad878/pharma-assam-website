1. Executive Summary & Core Platform Value

RepPulse Pharma SFA is an enterprise-grade Field Force Automation and Mobile CRM platform built specifically for pharmaceutical sales operations. Engineered to solve acute field challenges in multi-district territories, RepPulse enforces anti-fraud geotagged attendance, 15-minute background location telemetry, doctor/chemist visit auditing (DCR), Monthly Tour Plan (MTP) governance, and real-time managerial oversight.

The APK operates on a True Offline-First architecture: Medical Representatives (MRs) can log doctor calls, detail products, capture sample distributions, and book chemist orders even in remote hill pockets with zero cellular reception. When connectivity is restored, the synchronization engine flushes cached transactions to the Supabase cloud database using deterministic, collision-resistant event identifiers.

3. Core Operational Modules in the APK

3.1 Anti-Fraud Geotagged Attendance & Shift Lock

  • Geotagged Front-Facing Selfie: Captures fresh front-camera photo with real-time timestamping. Gallery selection is prohibited.
  • Hardware GPS Lock & Anti-Mock Engine: Inspects Android location provider flags (isMockLocation). Developer option location spoofing triggers immediate lockout.
  • 10:30 AM Shift Cutoff: Reps attempting to begin duty past 10:30 AM must submit a reason (e.g. transport delay). The request routes instantly to RSM Bodrud for digital authorization.

3.2 15-Minute Background Location Telemetry & Live Fleet Map

  • Background Telemetry: An optimized background worker captures rep GPS coordinates and battery percentage every 15 minutes during active shift hours.
  • Real-time Manager Map: RSMs and managers can open Team & Employees → Live Location to track active reps on an interactive map. If a rep has not started their shift, the system displays 'Shift Not Started'.

3.3 Daily Call Reporting (DCR) with 100-Meter Geofencing

  • Clinic Proximity Enforcement: Check-in is permitted only within 100 meters of the doctor's registered GPS coordinate using the mathematical Haversine formula.
  • Brand Detailing & Sampling: Logs discussed brands, sample drops (with batch numbers and quantities), and promotional materials.
  • Chemist POB Booking: Reps capture chemist orders, stockist tags, and booking values on the spot.

3.4 Monthly Tour Programme (MTP) & Calendar Sync

  • Pre-Planned Monthly Beats: Reps follow an approved calendar of daily routes across divisions stored in cloud PostgreSQL.
  • Auto-Default Working Beat: Scheduled town (e.g., Kalain Ex-station or Silchar Central HQ) automatically populates into daily attendance.
  • Mandatory Reschedule Approval: Reps cannot swap dates unilaterally. Any change requires submitting a formal request to the RSM.

3.5 Standard Fare Chart (SFC) & Travel Expenses

  • HQ Working: Standard TA ₹30–₹45, Daily Allowance (DA) ₹250/day.
  • Ex-station Working: Standard TA ₹135–₹195, Daily Allowance (DA) ₹450/day.
  • Outstation Working: Standard TA ₹420–₹540, Daily Allowance (DA) ₹750/day.
  • Receipt camera upload for hotel lodge charges, toll, and courier.
